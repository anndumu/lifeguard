        Three habits that keep scammers out

        Slow down

        Scammers rush you with deadlines and threats. A real company gives you time to check.

        Never pay with gift cards

        No real bank, government office, or grandchild asks for gift cards, wire transfers, or crypto.

        Ask someone you trust

        A quick call to family can stop a scam. Use "Ask family by text" after any check.

        How Lifeguard uses Google Gemini

        • Reads texts and screenshots. Paste a message or add a picture of it.
        • Listens to voicemails. Gemini writes down what the caller said, then checks it.
        • Gives a clear, structured answer. Every check comes back in the same simple format.
        • Quotes the exact warning signs. So we can highlight them right in the message.
        • Checks links with a tool. Gemini asks our link checker about each address it finds. We never open the link.
        • Answers in many languages. Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, Vietnamese, Korean, Tagalog, Arabic, French, and more.
        • Writes practice scams. New, made-up messages for the practice quiz every time.
        • Answers follow-up questions. Ask about the message you just checked.
